# Research integrity and use

This is a **working paper** presented as part of a prospective PhD research portfolio. The study reports recorded R-multiple outcomes and conditional statistical analysis, not independently audited trading performance.

The author should review and be able to defend the trade rules, dataset, statistical procedures, cited sources, and interpretation. Analytical and editorial tools, including AI assistance, may have supported preparation; disclose their use wherever an institution or publication requires it, and do not claim analyses were performed independently if they were not.

Public repository visibility means all uploaded data become accessible. Review trade records for sensitive information and verify that sharing is permitted before publication.
