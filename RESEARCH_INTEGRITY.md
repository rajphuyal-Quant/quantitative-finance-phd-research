# Research Integrity and Transparency

**Raj Phuyal | Independent Researcher, Quantitative Finance**  
**Research status: Working paper | October 2026**

## Purpose

I share this repository as part of my prospective PhD research portfolio. My current project examines rule-based intraday Nasdaq and Euro futures outcomes, with emphasis on statistical uncertainty, risk-normalized return asymmetry, and robust research design.

## Evidence and limitations

I analyze 242 recorded R-multiple outcomes across 180 trading dates in 2017–2018 and 2025–2026. I do not have the intervening 2019–2024 observations, so this is **not a continuous ten-year backtest**. The available records are not an independently verified record of live trading, an exhaustive reconstruction of eligible signals, or a demonstrated out-of-sample trading edge.

I distinguish the recorded descriptive findings from conditional statistical inference and proposed tests that still require independent historical prices, timestamps, rule documentation, execution costs, and pre-specified validation.

## Responsibility and attribution

I am responsible for understanding the research questions, reviewing the source records, checking the calculations and references, and ensuring that claims made under my name are accurate. I will not describe work as independently coded, audited, peer-reviewed, or externally validated unless that is supported by evidence.

I have used analytical and editorial assistance, including AI-assisted tools, in developing this research package. I review the outputs before presenting them as my work, and I will follow each university's or publication's applicable disclosure policy. Assistance with analysis or writing does not replace my obligation to understand and defend the methodology and conclusions.

## Reproducibility and data sharing

I provide recorded trade outcomes and a script that reproduces key descriptive statistics. The public script does **not** reproduce every test in the manuscript or verify trading executions. I state that boundary explicitly instead of claiming full reproducibility.

The research materials are shared for academic examination, not as investment advice or evidence of assured trading performance. I intend to correct documented errors transparently as the study develops.
